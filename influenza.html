Influenza – Preparing for Flu U.S. Season

A Data-Driven Case Study

Skills, Tools & Procedures

  • Skills: Business requirements translation, data cleaning & transformation, statistical analysis & forecasting, visual storytelling
  • Tools: Excel, Tableau
  • Procedures: Hypothesis testing, flu trend forecasting, visualizing mortality, insights presentation

Project Overview

Context: Every flu season, hospitals face staffing challenges due to increased patient load, especially among high-risk populations.

Key questions: Which states are most impacted? How do age, population density, and vaccinations affect staffing? When do flu peaks occur?

Why it matters: Preventing under- and overstaffing ensures efficient use of healthcare resources.

Key Insights

  • 90% of flu deaths occur in the 65+ age group
  • High-risk states include California, Florida, Texas
  • Low vaccination = Higher flu deaths
  • Higher population density = faster flu spread

Visualizing the Findings

Flu trends: Flu deaths fluctuate yearly & vary by region. Staffing must be flexible & data-driven.

Correlation: Aging populations show higher flu mortality. Staffing must align with demographic risk.

Recommendations & Next Steps

  • Prioritize high-risk states
  • Adjust staffing based on seasonal peaks
  • Target under-vaccinated regions
  • Develop early warning systems

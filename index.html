About Me

Hi, I’m Martin – a former industrial designer turned data analyst. My design background taught me to think visually and user-centered, which I now combine with a structured, analytical mindset.

During my CareerFoundry training, I tackled end-to-end data projects using Excel, SQL, Python, and Tableau – covering everything from data cleaning and visualization to regression and clustering. I’ve worked with real-world datasets from healthcare, e-commerce, and mobility sectors.

I’m driven by the idea of turning data into clear, human-centered decisions – and I’m seeking a Junior Data Analyst role in a purpose-driven, collaborative environment.

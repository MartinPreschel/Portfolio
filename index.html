About Me

Hi, I’m Martin – a former industrial designer who recently transitioned into data analytics. My design background taught me to think visually and user-centered, which I now combine with a structured, analytical mindset.

Through my CareerFoundry training, I conducted end-to-end projects using Excel, SQL, Python, and Tableau – covering everything from data cleaning and visualization to regression analysis and dashboards. I’ve worked with real-world data in areas like healthcare, e-commerce, and mobility, always aiming to turn data into meaningful, actionable insights.

What excites me most is using data to support clear, human-centered decisions. I’m now looking for a Junior Data Analyst role – ideally in a stable, purpose-driven environment where I can grow, collaborate, and contribute to impactful work.
